# 📅 When and Where will the Ipê Village take place?

**Where: Jurere internacional - Florianópolis - Brazil**

Jurerê Internacional is a luxurious beachfront neighborhood in Florianópolis, Brazil, renowned for its upscale resorts, vibrant nightlife, and exclusive lifestyle. Uniquely, it operates under private administration, ensuring high standards of maintenance, security, and amenities, attracting both tourists and residents seeking a premium living experience.

**When: from April 22nd to May 22nd**

The pop-up city will officially launch on April 22, a day rich in symbolism as both Brazil's Discovery Day and Earth Day, running through May 22.

This period is ideal for global visitors, strategically positioned between two major international events: AIBC Americas (April 7–10) and WebSummit Rio (April 27–30).
