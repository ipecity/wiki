# 🛠️ How To Collaborate?

We are seeking exceptional city builders from Brazil and worldwide who are committed to **prototyping a new city governance system** using AI, crypto, and other emerging technologies.

By contributing, in addition to building knowledge, experience, and a rich network, you will also earn **on-chain credentials, grants, tokens, and additional rewards**.

| **Role**          | **Description**                                                                      | **Requirements**                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Ipê Architect** | Bring your ideas, develop them solo or with a team, and present them during Demo Day | Ipê Village Pass + Attending at least 3 weeks |
| **Ipê Explorer**  | Engage in or organize meetups, events, conferences and workshops                     | Ipê Village Pass + Attending at least 5 days  |
| **Ipê Netizen**   | Participate in community activities and events                                       | Ipê Village Pass                              |
